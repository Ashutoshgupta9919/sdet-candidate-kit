# REVIEW.md

| # | Where | What is wrong | How to notice | How it was fixed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` | It treated MongoDB's naive UTC values as local shift time and did not anchor an overnight shift to its attendance date. | A UTC BSON punch at 09:40 IST is misread; an overnight punch after midnight can produce a negative delay. | Normalize instants to UTC, convert to IST, and compare with shift start on the attendance date. |
| 2 | `compute_late_minutes` | It tested the floored minute count against 10, so 09:40:01 was treated as inside the grace period. | On a 09:30 shift, 09:40:01 should return 10 late minutes. | Apply the strict 10-minute test to elapsed seconds, then floor elapsed whole minutes. |
| 3 | `compute_work_hours` | Python `round()` uses ties-to-even and binary floats; the contract requires decimal half-up rounding. | A duration of 18 seconds is an exact 0.005 hours and must round to 0.01. | Divide integer seconds with `Decimal` and `ROUND_HALF_UP`. |
| 4 | `compute_overtime` | It compared a timezone-aware shift end with a naive punch-out, omitted next-day shift ends, and counted any positive overtime. | Aware/naive values can raise `TypeError`; a 29-minute overrun must be zero and overnight shifts end next day. | Use IST-aware instants, advance overnight shift end, and apply the 30-minute threshold before flooring. |
| 5 | `EmployeeIn` | The starter accepted invalid employee codes, emails, dates, shift times, lengths, and equal shift boundaries. | Submit `emp_code="bad"`, malformed dates, or identical shift times. | Added contract pattern, length, calendar-date, email, and shift validation. |
| 6 | `PunchInIn` | It accepted non-presence statuses and weakly validated timestamps. | Try `status="LEAVE"`, a seconds timestamp, a float, or an out-of-range value. | Restricted status and added strict, bounded integer epoch-millisecond validation. |
| 7 | `health` | It always returned 200 without contacting MongoDB. | Stop MongoDB and request `/health`. | Ping MongoDB and return 503 until the database and indexes are available. |
| 8 | `create_employee` | A read-then-insert duplicate check is racy, and there was no unique index to enforce identity. | Send concurrent creates with one `emp_code`. | Added a unique index and convert duplicate-key conflicts to 409. |
| 9 | `create_employee` | `datetime.now()` used local naive time, and the response exposed a serialized datetime instead of epoch milliseconds. | Create an employee in a non-UTC host timezone and inspect `created_at`. | Store UTC datetimes and serialize instants to integer epoch milliseconds. |
| 10 | `list_employees` | `skip = page * page_size` skipped page one, `total` ignored the department filter, and results were unsorted. | Request page 1 with a department filter and compare item count with total. | Use one-based skip math, filtered counts, database sorting, and validated page bounds. |
| 11 | `punch_in` | Unknown employees caused a 500; local-time timestamp conversion, overnight dates, seconds truncation, and status validation were incorrect or absent. | Punch for an unknown code; submit UTC timestamps around midnight or an invalid status. | Return 404, normalize/truncate UTC instants, derive the IST attendance date, and validate the body. |
| 12 | `punch_in` | Check-then-insert allowed simultaneous requests to create duplicate attendance records. | Send two punch-ins for the same employee and date at once. | Added a unique compound index; the losing insert returns 409. |
| 13 | `list_attendance` | It fetched all matches into Python, sorted in memory, and did not break date ties by employee code. | Exercise a large collection and inspect stable ordering. | Apply filters, sort, count, skip, and limit in MongoDB with supporting indexes. |
| 14 | `list_attendance` | It returned an extra `id`, raw datetimes, and incomplete legacy records without required defaults. | Compare a response to `AttendanceRecord`, including a legacy document. | Serialize only contract fields, convert nested timestamps, and supply legacy defaults. |
| 15 | Application startup | No indexes were created, so uniqueness guarantees and explain-plan requirements were unmet. | Inspect indexes on a fresh database or run the explain checks. | Create the required employee and attendance indexes idempotently at startup. |
| 16 | Remaining API | Punch-out, regularization, four analytics endpoints, and explain were missing. | Request each path from the contract. | Implemented all required routes, with analytics expressed as MongoDB aggregation pipelines. |

The environment loading behavior was not a defect: `load_dotenv()` leaves real environment variables in priority. The `emp_code` field is the business identifier; MongoDB `_id` remains internal and is not returned by the API.
